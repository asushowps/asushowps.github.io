How to Play & Scoring

  • Fill horizontal lines to clear blocks and score points.
  • Clearing 4 lines simultaneously awards a high-bonus **Tetris!**
  • Swap difficult blocks into the Hold box using C or Hold button.
  • On mobile, use touch controls optimized for one-handed play.
